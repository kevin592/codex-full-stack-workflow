# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate constructively and assume good intent;
- focus criticism on ideas and code, not people;
- respect privacy, consent, and project boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and disruptive behavior;
- help keep technical discussions useful and accessible.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, or restrict
participation when behavior harms the community. Serious or sensitive concerns
may be reported privately through the repository security advisory channel.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and the safety of the
community.
